Scratch

Begin your adventure in digital making, by learning to code animations, stories, and games in Scratch.

Introduction to Scratch:

sprites, scripts, and loops

In this introduction to coding in Scratch for beginners, you will learn how to add code, costumes, and sounds to sprites as you make animations, a game, an app, and a book costumes, and sounds.

More Scratch:

broadcast, decisions and variables

More Scratch moves beyond the basics introduced in Introduction to Scratch. You will make apps, games and simulations using message broadcasting, if..then and if..then..else decisions and variables..

Further Scratch:

clones, my blocks, and boolean logic

Further Scratch moves beyond the skills introduced in Introduction to Scratch and More Scratch. You will make apps, games, computer-generated art, and simulations using boolean logic, functions, clones, and more.
